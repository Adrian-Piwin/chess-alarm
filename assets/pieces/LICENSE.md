# Piece artwork

The chess piece SVGs in this directory are the "Cburnett" set drawn by
[Colin M.L. Burnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User:Cburnett).

The originals on Wikimedia Commons (e.g. `Chess_klt45.svg`) are triple-licensed
under the GFDL, the **BSD licence** and the GPL. Chess Alarm uses them under the
BSD licence:

> Copyright (c) Colin M.L. Burnett. Redistribution and use in source and binary
> forms, with or without modification, are permitted provided that the above
> copyright notice and this permission notice are retained.

The files were taken from the lichess.org repository, which redistributes the
same artwork. `src/components/board/pieceSvgs.ts` is generated from them.
